Demo funds on the OKX demo account — a rehearsal of the strategy, not investment advice. The learning bot (BTC and ETH, each with its own position and stop, sharing one balance, at most 2 open at once): on every 4H close it looks for a trend pullback (with the EMA50/EMA200 trend, a dip to EMA20 that closes back) or a 20-candle breakout with the trend. A model trained on every earlier setup and how it ended — features from the ATLAS indicators and the BTC-Halving 200-week average and cycle — estimates the expected result, and the bot only trades setups it expects to win. It retrains on every new finished setup. 1% risk, initial stop 2×ATR, trailed 5×ATR behind the best close, no take-profit. Walk-forward test: research/LEARNING.md. State refreshes every minute from the repo; the live price every 10 s.
